Privacy Policy

For the RIME RUSH mobile app and related services · Effective October 5, 2026

RIME RUSH is an arcade climbing game. You can play without signing in. This policy explains what information is processed when you use online features.

Information the game processes

  • Local game data: settings, progress, scores, and other save data are stored on your device when you play without an account.
  • Account information: if you choose Google or X sign-in, the sign-in providers and RIME RUSH's authentication service process account identifiers and profile details needed to create and secure your account.
  • Online game activity: if you sign in or use online features, the game may process your display name, scores, floors reached, combos, achievements, and presence information to save results, show leaderboards, and connect players.
  • Technical data: application hosting, authentication, and database providers may process routine request and security logs, such as device/network information, to operate and protect the services.

How information is used

We use account and game information to sign you in, save and display your results, operate online features, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. The game does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Where information is processed

Online information is processed by the services that host and operate RIME RUSH, including its application hosting, authentication, and database providers. Google or X also process information when you choose their sign-in option. These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices and deletion

Sign-in is optional. You can play offline and keep game saves on your device. To ask us to access, correct, or delete online account information, email na0ryank0@gmail.com and include the sign-in method and account name. We may need to verify the request. Some records may remain in protected backups for a limited period or where required for security or law.

Children

RIME RUSH is an arcade game and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online account, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as RIME RUSH changes. The effective date above will be revised when we do. For privacy or support questions, visit RIME RUSH Support or email na0ryank0@gmail.com.
