Ascend the frozen monolith in RIME RUSH — the high-intensity vertical climbing arcade game!

Test your reflexes against the collapsing frost tower. Leap between brittle icy ledges, dodge falling icicles and hostile tundra hazards, and climb floor after floor in an endless rush for the ultimate summit score.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

⚡ RAPID-FIRE VERTICAL CLIMBING
Tight, responsive touch controls designed specifically for mobile. Tap and swipe to dash, wall-kick, and leap upward before the ice beneath your feet shatters.

🧗 EXPANSIVE CLIMBER ROSTER
Unlock over 12 unique climbers, custom scarf patterns, and distinctive color palettes. Equip weapons and gear to carve your path up the tower.

❄️ DYNAMIC FROST HAZARDS
Navigate through crumbling ledges, blizzard winds, falling hazards, and procedural obstacles that test your precision and timing on every ascent.

👥 REALTIME MULTIPLAYER & LEADERBOARDS
Compete against friends and global climbers in realtime matchmaking lobbies. Climb the global rankings, collect badges, and prove who rules the frost.

📱 OPTIMIZED FOR MOBILE
Seamless 60fps performance, immersive full-screen gameplay, haptic feedback, and battery-friendly architecture. Play solo offline or connect online to sync your progress across devices.
